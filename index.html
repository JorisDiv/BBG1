A Sweet History & Science

The World of Bubblegum 🍬

From chicle trees to world records: Exploring the elasticity, chemistry, and pop culture behind everyone's favorite treat.

1. The Accidental Invention

Bubblegum wasn't invented overnight! It took trial, error, and an accountant with a passion for chemistry.

  • 1928: Walter Diemer, an accountant for Fleer Chewing Gum Company, created the first successful bubblegum formula ("Dubble Bubble").
  • Why Pink? Pink was the only food coloring available at the factory at that moment!
  • Key Difference: Unlike standard chewing gum, bubblegum was less sticky and far more elastic.

2. The Science of Elasticity

Gum Base

Uses synthetic polymers (elastomers) that give bubblegum its unique stretchiness without dissolving in water.

Bubble Formation

Surface tension & film strength allow air to expand inside the gum matrix, creating light, stable bubbles!

3. Pop Culture & Records

  • Largest Bubble Ever: Blown by Chad Fell in 2004, measuring 20 inches (50.8 cm) in diameter without using his hands!
  • Baseball Tradition: Big League Chew (1980) became iconic as a safe alternative to chewing tobacco in dugouts.
  • Comic Strips: Bazooka Joe comics wrapped inside gum wrappers became collectible treasures for generations.
